Frequently Asked Questions

What is the cancellation policy?

We offer free cancellation up to 7 days before your booking. Cancellations within 7 days are subject to 50% deduction. No refunds for same-day cancellations.

What is the check-in and check-out time?

Standard check-in is at 2:00 PM and check-out is at 12:00 PM. Early check-in and late check-out can be arranged subject to availability (additional charges may apply).

Are pets allowed?

Yes, we welcome pets! Please inform us in advance so we can make suitable arrangements. Additional charges of ₹500 per day may apply.

Is Wi-Fi available?

Yes, high-speed Wi-Fi is available throughout the farmhouse at no additional cost. We also have backup internet connection for reliability.
